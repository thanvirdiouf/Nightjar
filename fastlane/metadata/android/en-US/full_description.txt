Nightjar helps you keep a private sleep journal using motion or sound intensity on your phone.

Track a night, explore activity-based phase estimates, add notes and tags, and compare weekly or monthly patterns. Set a wake-up deadline with an optional early window, or wind down with original offline ambient sounds.

Optional noise clips are recorded only after you opt in, stored locally, and removed according to your retention setting. Export CSV or JSON and make local backups.

No accounts, ads, cloud uploads or internet permission. Sleep phase labels and quality scores are unvalidated estimates, not medical measurements or diagnosis.
